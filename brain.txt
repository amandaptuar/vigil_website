API MODULES (apiService.js)
=======================================================
authApi:     getMe, login, logout, changePasswordFirstTime
childrenApi: getAll, create, updateName, delete, loginAndSendOtp, verifyOtpAndPair, getByEmail, activityOverview
callsApi:    getAll, getByType (incoming/outgoing/missed)
smsApi:      getAll, getInbox, searchByKeyword, getByAddress
filesApi:    getAll, getImages, getVideos
contactsApi: getAll
eventsApi:   getAll
locationApi: getLive, getHistoryByHours, getAllRecords
geofenceApi: getForChild, getForParent, create, toggle, delete
appsApi:     getAll
permissionsApi: getLiveStatus, getPermissions, updatePermissions, getDeviceInfo
aiApi:       runAnalysis, listDailyHistory, downloadReport, getRelationships, getLongitudinal
deviceApi:   getInfo (used by permissionsApi.getDeviceInfo)
